How to open this package

  1. Unzip the whole folder first. On Windows: right-click the .zip → Extract All. On a Mac: double-click the .zip. Opening files from inside the zip will not work.
  2. Keep the folders together: course, videos and reference must stay next to this file.
  3. Double-click START_HERE.html (this page). It opens in your web browser. Then click Open the course.

Phones and tablets: the course layout works on small screens, but most phones cannot open local web pages from a zip. To use it on a phone or tablet, put this folder on a web server or intranet site as plain files (no server code needed), or watch the MP4 videos and the PDF directly.

Important

The current DRS says how the work must be done, and it must be on hand where you work. Your site's cutsheet is the final word on any port. This training is built from the guide (cover Rev D, page footers Rev C, 10/8/2026) and the Fluidstack DRS sheets added so far (01.00.00 to 01.00.03.01). It is a training aid, never a substitute for the DRS. Where the sources are unclear or disagree, the course says so and tells you to ask your lead — see Open items.

Internal Use Only. The guide and the DRS are confidential. Keep this package within the project; do not post it publicly.
